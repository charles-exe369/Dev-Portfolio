## Overview

The Expense Tracker App helps users monitor their monthly budgets, track spending habits, and visualize income vs. expenses with interactive charts.

### Key Features

- **Real-time calculations:** Instant updates as expenses are added.
- **Categorization:** Tag items by food, housing, entertainment, or custom categories.
- **Dark Mode Support:** Clean, high-contrast user interface.
